About

Teaching models to see the structure of materials.

I'm a Ph.D. student in Engineering Science at the University of Mississippi and an NSF Graduate Research Fellow. My work sits where machine learning meets materials and manufacturing: self-supervised foundation models for crystallographic microstructure, next-frame prediction of additive manufacturing from 60,000 fps video, and ML-guided process optimization for metal and bio-printing.

Before that I earned a B.S. in Mechanical Engineering (summa cum laude), studied swarm robotics through an NSF REU at West Virginia University, and worked in vehicle calibration at PACCAR. Away from the lab I'm a professional photographer and a brown belt and volunteer instructor in Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu.
